Toasts

Toasts become a small floating card like the menus and popovers: card surface, hairline border, the shared soft shadow and a 12px corner. The only colour is the 16px icon. No filled green or red boxes. Text is a 13px title with an optional 12px grey line under it, and an action sits on the right as a small outline button.

Anatomy

  • Card: 360 wide, padding 12 (16 on the left), radius 12, 1px border, shadow-float. Same surface as menus and popovers.
  • Icon: 16px Remix line icon in a 16px column, 10px gap. Green check for done, red warning for failed, grey for everything else.
  • Title 13/18 medium. Description 12/16 grey, wraps, at most two short lines. No trailing periods on one-sentence lines.
  • Action: 28px outline button, 12px medium. Close: 24px ghost button, only on toasts that stay until dismissed.
  • Placement: bottom right, 24px from the edges, matching the page gutter. Stacks 8px apart and expands on hover.
  • Motion: slides up 8px and fades in, 200ms on the app's easing curve. Sonner handles enter, exit and stacking.
