What gets in the way

  1. 1Six buttons before any filter. Saved Filters, Save, Account lists, Reset, Export and Summary PDF stack up in three rows. Export is the only solid blue button, so the strongest thing in a filter panel is an action that isn't a filter.
  2. 2Two unlabelled icon buttons. Save and Account lists are a disk and a list icon. Neither says what it does until you hover.
  3. 3No overview of what's applied. To see your active filters you open each section and scroll. The only summary is a number at the bottom of the collapsed rail.
  4. 4Revenue is in raw millions. "1" to "716924" with "716,924M" underneath is hard to read. "Include all" really means "also include accounts with no revenue data", which the label doesn't say.
  5. 5Include and exclude rely on colour. The chips are green with + or red with −. For colour-blind users, or at a glance, "not India" and "India" look almost the same.
  6. 6Every field is always fully open. Each filter takes its full height even when unused, so Accounts alone runs past one screen. "Show More" hides another 8 Account and 4 Centre fields to compensate, and those are easy to forget.
  7. 7The collapsed rail doesn't show where filters are. The section icons look the same whether they have filters or not. The filter tile can't be clicked, and the count sits at the bottom (where the dev badge covers it locally).
  8. 8Option lists give no counts, and ranges come out of order. The data has a count per option, but it isn't shown. Headcount and revenue ranges are sorted by count, so "1K - 5K" comes before "<50". Saved Filters doesn't show which one is applied or whether you've changed it since.

Proposed

Click anything in the two panels: sections, fields, values, presets, saved filters, "Find a filter", collapse.

What changes, and why

  1. One header line: "Filters", the active count, Clear all and collapse. Reset becomes Clear all and is greyed out when nothing is applied.
  2. Saved filters becomes a labelled picker that shows the applied filter's name, with an orange dot when you've changed it since saving. Account lists stays as an icon button next to it, now with a tooltip naming it.
  3. "Find a filter" searches all 30 fields by name, so "City" or "NASSCOM" is one keystroke away instead of a scroll and a "Show More".
  4. Sections in sentence case with a count ("Accounts 4"), so you can see where the filters are before opening anything.
  5. Every field is a one-line row showing "Any" or its current value. Fields you use open in place; unused ones take 36px. The "More" fields sit in the same list under a divider, so "Show More" goes away.
  6. Exclude no longer relies on colour alone: excluded values carry a minus icon as well as the red tint, in chips, rows and lists. Clicking the icon still switches between include and exclude.
  7. Ranges read in real units ($1B to $50B, 1998 to 2015), with presets for common bands and a clear "Include accounts with no revenue data" switch in place of "Include all".
  8. Option lists show counts and keep ranges in order. Selected values are pinned on top, and Include/Exclude are labelled buttons with keyboard shortcuts.
  9. Export and Summary PDF move to a footer that's always visible, next to your profile. Export stays the primary button, but it no longer competes with the filters at the top.
  10. The collapsed rail shows counts per section and keeps saved filters, account lists, Export and PDF reachable without expanding.

Where the ideas come from

LinkedIn Sales NavigatorEvery filter offers Include and Exclude side by side, and excluded values are visibly different pills. The model behind items 7 and 9.
Apollo.ioIts filter sidebar is a list of one-line rows that open in place, each showing a count of what's set. The model behind item 6, and how it fits 30+ filters without "Show More".
Datadog facetsFacet values show their counts, the panel has a search box for facets, and collapsed groups still show what's selected. Item 8.
ZoomInfoSaved searches show when they were last run and whether results changed, with Save and Update kept together. Item 2 and the saved filters menu.
Linear and NotionA collapsed navigation rail that still shows counts and keeps the main actions reachable. Item 11.
